Practical Field Protocol

Take Safe-AWD to the Field

Three simple rules for Cauvery Delta cultivators to cut water use by 30% while protecting harvest yields.

01

Install ₹50 Pani Pipe

Sink a 30 cm perforated PVC pipe 20 cm deep into root zone at 15 DAT, leaving 10 cm above mud.

02

Re-Flood at -15 cm Depth

Let ponded water recede until tube reads -15 cm, then re-irrigate to +5 cm.

03

Hold Water at Flowering

Maintain 2–5 cm standing water during anthesis to protect grain set; drain 14 days before harvest.
